Context and Rationale

Challenges in Mapping Cerrado Pastures

The high spatial and ecological heterogeneity of pastures in the Cerrado biome demands innovative methodologies combining high-resolution remote sensing, dense spectral representations, and rigorous in situ validation.

Territorial Sampling
62,395 Annual Samples across the Cerrado

Continuous multi-year historical series combining stratified samples (50k Series) and pasture collection filters (12k Series) from 2019 to 2025, totaling 438,200 observations for land use transition dynamics.

Spectral Representation
Sentinel-2 Embeddings (64 Dimensions)

Multidimensional latent space at 10-meter spatial resolution, synthesizing seasonal phenological shifts, vegetative vigor, soil exposure, and canopy texture.

Similarity & Validation
Cosine Similarity with Embrapa Ground Truth

Massive dot-product computation between the sample points and Embrapa's 701 ground-truth points, determining maximum similarity matches and the Md3 concordance metric.
