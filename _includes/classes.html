Pasture Typologies

Embrapa Classification Scheme

701 in situ field reference points surveyed and characterized by Embrapa across the Cerrado biome, classified into 7 canonical typologies with quantitative vegetation parameters (forage grass, woody species, exposed soil, weeds/ruderals, and altitude).

Productive Pasture
High forage grass coverage, vigorous density, and absence of severe degradation.
Pasture with Weeds
Pasture with moderate to severe infestation of herbaceous and ruderal invasive plants.
Pasture with Shrubs / Woody Species
Significant presence of shrub and tree canopy layers across the pasture area.
Intermediate Pasture
Intermediate vegetative vigor stage, with mixed coverage of forage and bare soil.
Biological Degradation
Prominent bare soil, visible erosion signs, and severe loss of carrying capacity.
Natural Regeneration
Areas undergoing natural vegetative recovery and succession of native Cerrado physiognomies.
Miscellaneous
Atypical, transitional, or mixed compositions that do not fit into standard categories.
