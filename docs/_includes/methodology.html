The pipeline structures a progressive, verifiable sample expansion framework designed for the new 10 m pasture mapping of the Cerrado biome. For each of the 701 Embrapa Cenargen field polygons (approximately 1 ha; 608 classified across 7 pasture condition categories and 93 unlabeled) and the 62,009 candidate points (50,000 persistent Sentinel-2 points, 5,453 MapBiomas Col. 4 S2 points, and 6,556 Landsat validation points), two complementary representation spaces are organized across 2020–2025:

1. Monthly Sentinel-2 Time Series: Extracted on Google Earth Engine using a centered moving window of ~60 days (the second half of the previous month, the full reference month, and the first half of the following month). The monthly value is calculated as the joint median of all cloud-free, valid pixel-date pairs (covering NDVI, EVI, NDII, NDWI, PSRI, and CAI).
2. Annual Latent Embeddings (AlphaEarth Foundations): 64-dimensional annual vectors (A00–A63) representative of the ~1 ha spatial support. Because the spatial average of unit vectors does not preserve a unit norm, explicit L2-renormalization is enforced before calculating cosine similarity against the 608 classified Embrapa references.

Candidates approved through embedding similarity (evaluating Top-1/Top-3 nearest neighbors, concordance score md3, margin separation, and MapBiomas Col. 11 agreement) undergo a second filtering stage in a 12×12 Self-Organizing Map (SOM). The SOM evaluates spectral-temporal coherence using Dirichlet-Multinomial Bayesian smoothing, categorizing samples into keep, forward for inspection, or reject. Pre-selected subsets undergo high-resolution visual quality control using pan-sharpened CBERS-4A imagery (~2 m) under a blind, multi-interpreter protocol before feeding the Random Forest models and undergoing subsequent image- and field-based validation (~100 localities, prioritizing Goiás).
