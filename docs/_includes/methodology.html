The pipeline processes Sentinel-2 (10 m) satellite imagery on Google Earth Engine with automated cloud masking and multispectral metric extraction throughout the rainy season in the Cerrado biome. Each sample point is assigned a 64-dimensional embedding vector (A00 to A63), capturing the vegetation's structural and phenological signature.

Leveraging the high-performance DuckDB analytical engine, each of the annual sample points is compared via dot product (cosine similarity) against all 701 Embrapa field reference points. The algorithm ranks the top-matching references, extracts the corresponding typology, and computes the Mean Similarity Concordance (Md3), enabling multi-year temporal monitoring from 2019 to 2025.
