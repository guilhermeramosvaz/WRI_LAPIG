Pasture Condition Categories

Embrapa Cenargen Classification Scheme

701 in situ field reference areas (~1 ha polygons) surveyed by Embrapa Cenargen across the Cerrado biome. 608 samples possess a defined label across seven pasture condition categories, while 93 unlabeled units are retained for unsupervised steps (such as SOM training).

Productive Pasture (Pasto Produtivo)
High forage grass coverage, vigorous density, and absence of severe degradation signs.
Pasture with Weeds (Pasto com Ervas)
Pasture with moderate to severe infestation of herbaceous and ruderal invasive plants.
Pasture with Shrubs (Pasto com Lenhosas)
Significant presence of shrub and woody canopy layers across the pasture support area.
Intermediate Pasture (Intermediário)
Intermediate vegetative vigor stage, with mixed coverage of forage and bare soil.
Biological Degradation (Deg. Biológica)
Prominent bare soil, visible erosion signs, and severe loss of carrying capacity.
Natural Regeneration (Reg. Natural)
Areas undergoing natural vegetative recovery and succession of native Cerrado physiognomies.
Miscellaneous (Miscelânea)
Atypical, transitional, or mixed compositions that do not fit into standard categories.
