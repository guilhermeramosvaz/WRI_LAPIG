Context and Rationale

Challenges in Mapping Cerrado Pastures & Sample Curation

The high spatial, temporal, and ecological heterogeneity of pastures in the Cerrado biome demands a traceable strategy to expand sample sets from high-quality field references, filter pseudo-labeling errors in complementary representation spaces, and rigorously document uncertainty.

Sample Expansion at Scale
62,009 Candidates across the Cerrado

Structured universe combining 5,453 Sentinel-2 Col. 4 points, 6,556 Landsat validation points, and 50,000 persistent pasture points (2020–2025) stratified across low (~35%), medium (~44%), and high (~21%) vigor. Sensitivity analysis comparing inclusive universe vs. 44,943 points corroborated by Collection 11.

Dual Representation Spaces
64-Dim Embeddings & S2 Time Series

AlphaEarth Foundations annual embeddings (64 latent dimensions at 10 m) combined with monthly Sentinel-2 spectral series (NDVI, EVI, NDII, NDWI, PSRI, CAI) extracted via a 60-day moving window using the joint median of valid pixel-date pairs.

Quality Control & Validation
Cosine Similarity, SOM & CBERS-4A

L2-renormalized cosine similarity against 608 classified Embrapa reference polygons, Top-1/Top-3 md3 concordance, 12×12 SOM clustering with Dirichlet-Multinomial Bayesian smoothing, and stratified ~2 m CBERS-4A visual inspection before Random Forest training.
