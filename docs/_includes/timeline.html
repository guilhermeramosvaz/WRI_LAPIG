Project Workflow

Methodological Stages

Stage 01
Sentinel-2 Embedding Extraction
Data cube processing and 64-dimensional annual vector generation (2019 to 2025) on Google Earth Engine.
Stage 02
Geolocation & Temporal Partitioning
Geographic coordinate alignment (lat_target, lon_target) and division of records into structured annual datasets.
Stage 03
Dot-Product Similarity Computation
High-throughput streaming execution in DuckDB comparing samples against Embrapa's 701 ground-truth points.
Stage 04
Top-3 Ranking & Md3 Metric
Pivoting top similarity matches, assigning pasture typologies, and computing the mean concordance score (Md3).
Stage 05
Web Optimization & Interactive Viewer
Data matrix compression and high-performance browser rendering via GPU-accelerated Leaflet Canvas.
Stage 06
Cross-Analysis & Territorial Documentation
Concordance evaluation against MapBiomas collections, threshold filters, and technical report delivery.
